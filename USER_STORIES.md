# Hebelwerk — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Strategy secretariat coordinator (coordinating unit, shared across the three lead ministries)

- As a secretariat coordinator, I want to register each field of action and the measures under it with a named lead ministry and unit, so that the twelve fields stop being chapter headings and become an owned list.
- As a secretariat coordinator, I want to classify every measure as firm, conditional or advocacy, so that when the minister asks how much of the strategy is actually committed I can answer with three numbers instead of one misleading one.
- As a secretariat coordinator, I want a single view of measures whose delivery depends on a third party we cannot direct — the Länder, the institute's shareholders, the standardisation bodies, the social partners — so that I can see the share of the strategy that is contingent by construction.
- As a secretariat coordinator, I want the coordination report to show measures with no current owner and spillover assessments nobody answered, so that coordination failure is a visible figure rather than an anecdote from the last meeting.

### Ministry measure owner (Referat lead in a lead ministry)

- As a measure owner, I want to record milestones against my measure with the instrument type that delivers it, so that "negotiations opened with the Länder" counts as progress on a negotiated measure rather than looking like a missed spend.
- As a measure owner, I want to file a spillover assessment naming the portfolios my measure touches, so that I discharge the duty to assess cross-portfolio impact with a dated record instead of a paragraph in a meeting note.
- As a measure owner, I want to declare slippage with a stated cause and say explicitly whether I am moving the date, the target or the scope, so that a delay does not quietly become a redefinition.
- As a measure owner, I want to see the counting definition in force for my target before I report a number, so that I am not credited with progress I would have to withdraw when the definition is checked.

### Budget officer (budget directorate of a lead ministry)

- As a budget officer, I want every funding line tagged as newly appropriated, already in the financial plan, reallocated within our individual budget plan, or notionally attributed from the research and development uplift, so that no press release turns pre-existing money into a new announcement.
- As a budget officer, I want the ledger to refuse a combined total that mixes provenance classes without displaying the split, so that a figure quoted from our system cannot be used to overstate what the Federation has newly put on the table.
- As a budget officer, I want each year of a multi-year measure to be marked as authorised or merely planned, so that a commitment running to 2025 is never presented as if the later years were already appropriated.

### Länder and co-financing liaison

- As a liaison officer, I want to record a co-financing pledge from a Land, a research organisation or an industry partner, then separately record whether it was contracted and realised, and have the leverage report keep confirmed and unconfirmed contributions apart, so that the doubling claim rests on signed contributions rather than the goodwill expressed in a communiqué, and a Land which delivered is not lumped in with one that only announced.
- As a liaison officer, I want to attempt to log a leverage statement that counts an unconfirmed pledge towards the doubling target and have the system reject it with the confirmed figure it will accept instead, so that an unverified claim cannot reach a published document through our own ledger.

### Parliamentary and audit liaison

- As a parliamentary liaison, I want to answer a Study Commission question directly from the ledger with the records the answer relied on attached, so that a follow-up question three months later gets the same figure.
- As an audit liaison, I want to show the auditors the provenance split, the definition amendment history and the slippage records for a measure, so that a value-for-money examination reads a documented decision trail instead of reconstructing one from correspondence.
- As a governance administrator, I want to run a portfolio reorganisation that re-owns every affected measure inside a transition window while preserving the original assignment, so that a machinery-of-government change does not erase the accountability history of the measures it moves.
